Resolution

On returns, the data points to a targeted fix rather than a blanket one: tightening COD verification specifically for Tier-2 cities should address the 54.5% return rate concentrated there, without penalizing the much lower-risk Card and UPI channels or COD orders from Tier-1 cities.
